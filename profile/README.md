# edit.legal

edit.legal is an AI legal assistant for lawyers, in-house counsel, and legal teams working with Italian law. Users ask legal questions in natural language and receive answers with citations to the codes, the Court of Cassation, regional administrative courts, and the Council of State, drawn from about one million legislative and case-law sources updated daily.

A collaborative legal document editor with AI suggestions and more than 50 templates supports drafting and is also available as a Microsoft Word add-in. Data is stored in data centers in the European Union. A 7-day free trial is available with no credit card required.

## In italiano

edit.legal è un assistente di intelligenza artificiale per avvocati, uffici legali e team legali che lavorano con il diritto italiano. Si pone una domanda giuridica in linguaggio naturale e si ottiene una risposta con le citazioni delle fonti: codici, Cassazione, TAR e Consiglio di Stato.
